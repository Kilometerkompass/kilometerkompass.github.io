In Brief

Kilometerkompass does not send any data to the app’s developer. What you enter stays on your device and – if you use iCloud – in your private iCloud storage. The developer cannot see this data. The app contains no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics tools.

What Data the App Processes

  • Contract data: vehicle name, start of contract and term, mileage allowance, odometer reading at collection, rates for excess and unused mileage, tolerances, forecast and reminder settings, and, if you wish, monthly payment, initial payment and additional costs, fuel type, consumption and energy price, as well as your notes.
  • Readings: date, odometer reading and note.
  • Maintenance appointments: type, name, when due by date or odometer reading, interval and when last done.
  • Cost items: type, name, amount, how often it occurs, whether it belongs to the lease or to running costs, whether it is a credit and, for one-off items, the date.
  • Settings: appearance, app lock, the chart view you last selected and when the app last asked for a rating.

The app stores this data on your device. It is used solely to show you where you stand on your lease contract.

Syncing via iCloud

If iCloud is turned on for your device, the app also stores your data in your private iCloud database so that it is available on your other devices. Apple handles storage and transmission. So that changes made on another device arrive straight away, Apple sends the app silent push notifications. The app’s developer has no access to your private iCloud database. Apple’s privacy policy applies (https://www.apple.com/uk/legal/privacy/).

You can turn off syncing in your device’s Settings under your name → iCloud.

Camera and Photos

Using the camera, the app can read your odometer. The live image is analysed solely on your device; it is neither stored nor transmitted. The app asks for your permission first, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device’s settings.

If you choose an existing photo instead, the app sees only that one photo. It, too, is analysed on the device and not stored.

Face ID, Touch ID and Device Passcode

The app lock is optional. iOS carries out the check; the app only learns whether it was successful. The app does not receive any biometric data.

Reminders

Reminders about readings that are due, the end of your contract and maintenance appointments are notifications that the app schedules on your device. Nothing is transmitted to a server for this.

Widgets

So that the widgets can show where you stand, the app places a copy of your contracts and readings in a protected area on your device that only the app and its widgets can read. The copy does not leave the device. If the lock with Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode is turned on in the app, the widgets on the Lock Screen and in StandBy show no figures, only the name of the car.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use the “Log Odometer Reading” shortcut with Siri, Apple processes your voice input under its own terms. The app receives only the recognised odometer reading and the contract you chose.

Export

You share readings as a spreadsheet or a report only if you tap “Share” yourself. You decide where the file goes. The same applies to “Recommend Kilometerkompass” in the settings: the app only passes on a short text with the link to this website.

Asking for a Rating

After you have bought the full version and logged a few readings, the app occasionally asks for a rating on the App Store using Apple’s rating prompt. iOS itself limits how often the prompt appears. For this, the app only counts how many readings you have logged since the last request and when it last asked; this stays on your device. If you leave a rating, Apple processes it under its own terms. The developer sees it only as it is published on the App Store.

Free Trial

When you start the free trial, the app triggers a free purchase through the App Store. Apple records the time of this purchase in your Apple Account so that the free trial applies equally on all your devices and does not start over after a reinstall. The developer learns nothing about you in the process.

Purchase of the Full Version

The in-app purchase is handled entirely through Apple’s App Store. The developer does not receive any payment details. Apple only provides the developer with aggregated sales figures that cannot be linked to individual people.

Crash Reports

If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” on your device under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple provides the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. They help to find bugs. The setting is up to you.

Storage Period and Deletion

Your data remains stored until you delete it in the app or remove the app. You can delete the copy in iCloud in your device’s iCloud settings under “Manage Account Storage” → KM-Kompass.

This Website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., USA. When you visit them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page and protect it against attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the pages securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub’s privacy policy applies (GitHub Privacy Statement). The pages themselves set no cookies, load nothing from other providers and do not analyse your visit.

The mileage calculator works entirely in your browser. What you enter there is transmitted neither to the developer nor to GitHub, nor is it stored; when you close the page, it is gone.

The pages link to Kilometerkompass on the App Store. Safari on iPhone and iPad therefore shows a banner at the top with a button to download the app; Safari itself obtains the information for it from Apple, not this page. You only reach the App Store when you tap one of these links. Apple’s privacy policy applies there.

Support Requests

If you email or call the developer, your contact details and your request are used only to reply to you and are deleted once the request has been dealt with, unless a statutory retention obligation prevents this. The legal basis is the handling of your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it concerns a purchase, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The email address is a mailbox at Apple iCloud Mail.

Transfers to the USA

Apple and GitHub are based in the USA or belong to companies based there. Insofar as data reaches the USA in the process, the transfer is based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which both companies have joined (Art. 45 GDPR), and additionally on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and, in the UK, the UK GDPR, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority – for example with the authority responsible for the developer, the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany). In the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). As the developer does not store any data about you, all your data is in your hands; the app can export it as a spreadsheet at any time. If you have any questions, you can reach the developer at the address given above.

Changes

If the app changes in such a way that this policy no longer fits, it will be updated. The version published here at any given time applies.
