Summary

Kilometerkompass is an iPhone app for anyone who drives a car with a mileage allowance – on personal contract hire (PCH), a PCP with a mileage limit or another lease. From the contract details and the odometer readings taken, it works out at any time whether you are above or below the mileage allowance and what that means at the end of the contract: an excess mileage charge or a refund for unused mileage, in pounds, euros or Swiss francs, with distances in miles or kilometres. Rates, tolerances and a refund cap are entered just as they appear in the contract. The app logs the odometer reading by camera, with Siri or with one tap on its start screen. It also works out what the car costs in total – monthly payments, initial payment, insurance, vehicle tax, electricity or fuel and the expected mileage settlement, also per month and per mile or kilometre – reminds you of services, tyre changes and the MOT, and helps with the return and with choosing the next lease. The data stays on the device and in the user’s private iCloud – no account, no ads, no tracking.

Features

Analyse

  • Where you stand at a glance: distance driven, target to date, the share of the mileage allowance used and what it all means at the end of the contract, as an excess mileage charge or a refund.
  • Forecast to the end of the contract: based on recent weeks, on the whole contract so far or on your own annual mileage – plus the figures if you don’t drive any further.
  • Calculates like the contract: rates for excess and unused mileage, tolerances as a free margin or as a threshold, a refund cap as a distance or an amount; after the end of the contract, the final settlement.
  • What Does a Trip Cost? Enter a distance and see what it will cost at the end of the contract, including electricity or fuel – and with several cars, which one makes the trip cheapest.
  • Monthly budget: how far you can drive this month to end up exactly on the mileage allowance.
  • Total cost: monthly payment, initial payment, delivery and registration, plus insurance, vehicle tax, maintenance package and your own items, credits for money you get back, and electricity or fuel from consumption and price – in mpg or mi/kWh with miles. The app works out the cost over the term, including the expected mileage settlement, split into lease, energy, maintenance and running costs, per month and per mile or kilometre.
  • History and readings: a chart with distance driven, target and forecast; readings listed by month.

Log

  • Odometer reading by camera: point the camera at the odometer and tap the reading, or choose an existing photo; the image is analysed on the iPhone only.
  • Logged in seconds: with one tap on the start screen, with Siri, Spotlight and the Shortcuts app, from the app icon’s menu, in Control Centre or with the Action button.
  • Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen, a separate one for every car.
  • Reminders for the next reading and, if you like, four weeks before the end of the contract.

Manage

  • As many contracts as you like, with an overview of them all together.
  • Maintenance: service, tyre change, MOT and your own appointments, by date or by odometer reading. The app estimates when an odometer reading will be reached and sends a reminder two weeks before.
  • Return checklist: what is due three months, four weeks and one week before the return, on the day and afterwards, with dates matching the end of the contract.
  • Plan Next Lease: which mileage allowance suits the distance you really drive, and which of two offers ends up cheaper – monthly payments, initial payment, delivery, maintenance and mileage settlement.
  • A contract as a template: the next contract takes over the term, rates, tolerances and reminders from the old one.
  • Sync via iCloud: contracts and readings are stored in the user’s private iCloud.
  • App lock with Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode; Lock Screen widgets then show no figures.
  • Export: readings as a spreadsheet (CSV) and the current position as a report.
  • Languages and units: English, German, French, Italian and Spanish; miles or kilometres; pounds, euros or Swiss francs. Nothing is converted.
  • No account, ads or tracking: the developer sees no user data.

Background

With a car lease, many people only find out where they stand when they hand the car back. Kilometerkompass answers the question along the way: at the current rate, are you above or below target – and what will it mean in pounds or euros if things carry on like this? On the website, a free Mileage Calculator does the same for a single odometer reading, right in the browser and without signing up. Kilometerkompass is developed and published by Christian Lehnert.
